Open infrastructure, built in public

Your cooperative owns its work.
It should own its institutional memory too.

The InterCooperative Network is shared digital infrastructure for cooperatives, communities, and federations. It connects the rules an institution sets, the decisions it makes under them, the actions that follow, and the durable proof of why — instead of scattering those across six products owned by somebody else.

See ICN work Start reading

Under active construction, and honest about it — what's real now states what each part of the system can and cannot do today.

The problem

An institution's memory is scattered across tools that don't know about each other.

Every cooperative already has governance, rules, membership, money, and work. What it usually doesn't have is any system that holds those together — so the connections live in people's heads, and leave when they do.

The deeper version of the problem

Underneath the operational friction is a dependency problem. Democratic institutions are expected to run their internal life on systems controlled by outside owners, with outside incentives and outside priorities. A cooperative can own its workplace outright and still rent its nervous system.

Why this is structural, not just inconvenient

What this looks like in practice

A forty-person cooperative needs to replace a fan.

How it usually goes

  1. Someone raises it in the shop channel. It scrolls away.
  2. It comes back at a meeting. The minutes land in a shared drive.
  3. A vote happens in a separate tool. The result is pasted back into chat.
  4. Someone orders the fan. The invoice goes to the bookkeeper.
  5. Eighteen months later, an auditor asks who authorized it.
  6. Three people spend an afternoon reconstructing the answer.

With ICN

  1. The cooperative already has a rule about who decides safety spending.
  2. The item appears as work needing a decision, with a deadline.
  3. The person acting can see exactly what authorizes them to act.
  4. Before confirming, they see what record it will create, and whether it can be undone.
  5. Completing it produces a receipt that points back at the decision.
  6. Eighteen months later, the answer is one link, not an afternoon.

Nothing above removes the meeting, the argument, or the judgement. The people still decide. What changes is that the decision stops evaporating the moment it's made.

What changes

Rules stop being documents people are expected to remember, and start being the thing that determines what can happen next.

That is the whole shift, and it is narrower than "ICN fixes democratic institutions." Deliberation, politics, judgement, and responsibility stay with people. What the infrastructure contributes is memory, continuity, and the ability for an institution to be legible to itself — so that running a cooperative rests on something more durable than whoever is currently holding the pieces together.

It also means an institution can answer questions about its own past without depending on a vendor to still exist, still export, and still be telling the truth.

The worked example

Watch one decision travel the whole loop.

A short guided walkthrough of a fictional cooperative: who counts as a member, what needed deciding, which rule permitted it, what would happen before anything happened, and the record that can still explain it a year later.

No account, no download, no command line. Around four minutes. Every value in it is invented, and the page says so at every step.

Current state

What is actually built, and how we know.

ICN's subsystems are at very different stages, and the project publishes that unevenness rather than averaging it into a reassuring summary. Two labels travel with every claim: how far along it is, and what kind of evidence sits behind it.

6 of 10 subsystems in the strong today band

Economics (Mutual Credit Ledger), Entity Model, Governance, Identity & Cryptography, Network & Transport, Security & Privacy.

1 still not yet

Distributed Compute — scaffolding exists, the capability does not.

2026-08-14 last verified against source

What none of this means

ICN has no production deployment claim and no live federation. A subsystem can be well-built and still only ever have been exercised against prepared data — several are. Evidence labels like Fixture-backed exist precisely so that distinction survives contact with a homepage.

Taking part

Now that you know what this is.

Four routes, each leading somewhere that is actually maintained. If a path below turns out to be a dead end, that is a bug and we would like to hear about it.

You write software

Build the workspace, read the architectural guardrails, and pick a scoped first issue. The repository is large and the contribution surface is real.

For developers

You organize, write, research, or design

Documentation, plain-language review, governance thinking, accessibility testing, translation. None of this requires writing Rust, and the project is short on all of it.

Non-technical contribution

You are part of a cooperative or institution

Understand what would actually be involved in evaluating ICN, what it can carry today, and what it plainly cannot. Honest answers before any commitment.

For cooperatives and institutions

You want to follow or support the work

Watch the repository for project updates, or sustain the engineering directly. No mailing list you would have to trust us to actually send.
