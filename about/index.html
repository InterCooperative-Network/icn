About the Project

InterCooperative Network

Cooperatives built on extractive platforms are still extractive at the infrastructure layer. ICN is the substrate that fixes that — democratic governance, cooperative economics, and self-sovereign identity running on P2P infrastructure that no one owns but everyone runs.

Try it now → See the roadmap

What is ICN?

ICN is a P2P coordination daemon — a background service that connects with other ICN nodes to form cooperative networks. It provides four foundational institution primitives:

🔑
Identity & Membership

Self-sovereign DIDs with Ed25519 cryptography. Web-of-participation trust. Your members own their identity.

📜
Rules & Governance

CCL contracts encode bylaws as executable code. Proposals, votes, receipts, delegation — democratic decisions that scale.

💰
Economics

Mutual credit ledger with Merkle-DAG integrity. Dynamic credit limits. Cooperative treasury without extractive finance.

🌐
Interoperation

Federation agreements, cross-group trust, clearing, and shared resource allocation across cooperative networks.

How is it different?

Traditional ICN
Governance Bureaucratic / Centralized Programmable democracy (CCL)
Economics Extractive finance Mutual credit + cooperative treasury
Identity Platform accounts Self-sovereign DIDs
Trust Reputation scores Attestation-based trust graph
Infrastructure Central servers / blockchain P2P daemon — no mining, no landlord
