Live on K3s

ICN Demo: Five Cooperative Flows

Five demonstration organizations on a live K3s cluster. Five end-to-end flows covering governance, economics, federation, reporting, and compute. Each flow produces cryptographic receipts — running against real infrastructure, not a sandbox.

Prerequisites

kubectl access to the K3s cluster. Run demo/scripts/reseed-federation-demo.sh before any demo day to seed identity, trust, and state.

Each flow accepts --present (minimal output, pause on key beats) or --narrated (full explanatory output) flags.

1

Cooperative Governance

Brightworks Collective
~8 min

A member proposes expanding the cooperative's digital services mandate. The full governance loop runs: proposal submission, member voting, tally, and outcome — each step producing a verifiable receipt.

Proves: Proposal lifecycle Democratic vote GovernanceReceipt decision_hash provenance 
bash demo/scripts/flow-1-governance.sh [--present | --narrated]
2

Patronage Settlement

Brightworks Collective
~10 min

After a governance decision, the cooperative distributes patronage dividends to members. The settlement is anchored to the governance receipt via decision_hash — proving the economic outcome flows directly from the democratic decision.

Proves: Patronage allocation Settlement receipt Governance → ledger provenance 
bash demo/scripts/flow-2-patronage.sh [--present | --narrated]
3

Clearinghouse & Mutual Credit

Rochester Cooperative Clearinghouse
~8 min

The clearinghouse cooperative records treasury activity across member organizations. Mutual credit positions, ledger entries, and cross-cooperative clearing are demonstrated without a central bank or settlement authority.

Proves: Mutual credit ledger Cross-coop clearing Treasury position 
bash demo/scripts/flow-3-clearinghouse.sh [--present | --narrated]
4

Regulatory Reporting

Harbor Freight Workers Cooperative
~8 min

Harbor generates a signed compliance report over its transaction history. The report is anchored to on-chain governance receipts, enabling third-party verification without exposing internal financials.

Proves: Report generation Transaction audit trail Signed governance anchor 
bash demo/scripts/flow-4-reporting.sh [--present | --narrated]
5

Commons Compute

Finger Lakes CDN
~5 min

Finger Lakes CDN submits a compute task to the commons pool. Two gates are enforced before admission: a compute:write scope check on the capability token, and a trust score check against the commons admission threshold (MIN_TRUST_SUBMIT = 0.1). Both pass. The task enters the queue with a Blake3 content hash as its provenance anchor.

Sprint 28 complete: Gossip fan-out fix enables task dispatch via loopback. CCL executor is live. Tasks move Pending → Completed. Settlement receipts with full provenance chain are generated on completion.
Proves: Trust-gated admission compute:write scope Ledger position query Authorization boundary 
bash demo/scripts/flow-5-compute.sh [--present | --narrated]

What Comes Next

Distributed Execution

Multi-executor node pool scaling across K3s cluster. Tasks already execute via gossip loopback; this expands to parallel execution across cooperative-contributed nodes.

Mobile UX

Member-facing PWA for governance participation, patronage tracking, and cooperative membership management.

Federation Treaties

Formal inter-cooperative agreements with treaty-based governance and mutual credit clearing across organizations.
