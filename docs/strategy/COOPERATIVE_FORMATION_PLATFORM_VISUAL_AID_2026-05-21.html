60-second opener

From what I understand, Launch is trying to make co-op formation less like wandering through a legal, financial, and governance junk drawer with a flashlight dying in your mouth.

You are breaking the process into steps people can actually move through: teams, documents, tasks, advisors, service providers, and milestones.

ICN is coming at the next layer: once those steps become real institutional facts, how does the co-op remember them? Who has standing? Who was authorized? What decision happened? What obligation followed? What evidence survives when the advisor, organizer, or founding team rotates out?

I am not here to pitch ICN as a replacement for Launch. I want to understand whether there is a clean seam between Launch's formation workflow and ICN's durable governance / memory layer — or whether that seam does not exist yet.

Primary walkthrough — formation → durable governance handoff

Walk one beat at a time. After each beat ask: How does this happen today? What state needs to persist? Who needs to trust it later? Where would a receipt help, and where would it become bureaucracy?

  1. Group enters Launch onboarding
    TodayDecision-tree intake / wizard.
    ICNFirst institutional record that this group exists and is forming?
  2. Founding team / co-founders identified
    TodayRoster inside Launch.
    ICNWhen do these people gain standing — and what kind?
  3. Roles & advisor / TA / provider relationships established
    TodayRelationship records in Launch.
    ICNWho can act with what authority, on whose behalf, with what professional scope?
  4. Formation path selected
    TodayCo-op model / legal entity track chosen.
    ICNWhat process is this group in, and what authority does that selection carry?
  5. Governance documents drafted
    TodayDrafts in document portal.
    ICNUnder whose authority, and what receipt when adopted?
  6. Tasks assigned to co-founders / advisors / providers
    TodayTask surface in Launch.
    ICNEach task is an obligation — by whom, to whom, owed by when, completed by what evidence?
  7. Legal / accounting / co-op-developer service provider loop triggered
    TodayVendor / advisor engagement, often outside Launch.
    ICNWhat crosses the boundary, what comes back, what record proves both?
  8. Decision or milestone completed
    TodayChecked box / signed doc / meeting minute.
    ICNWhat effect did it have, against what authority, with what receipt?
  9. Member standing / board authority / operating roles established
    TodayThe moment Launch hands off to "the co-op exists now."
    ICNWhat carries forward — and what is silently dropped?
  10. Economic participation / patronage / allocation records become relevant
    TodayUsually CPA / bookkeeper territory.
    ICNWhere does governance evidence meet accounting record? See economic branch below.
  11. Handoff into durable institutional memory / ongoing governance
    TodayFuzzy moment where Launch's role recedes.
    ICNWhat record survives the handoff that next year's board, a funder, a regulator, or a future member can verify?

Questions to ask

On Launch's workflow
Where does a formation workflow lose institutional memory?
What records matter after the co-op is formed?
Where do teams / tasks / docs / advisor notes need continuity?
What milestones are legally / accounting- / governance-meaningful?
On standing / authority
When does someone become a member / founder / board actor / officer / advisor?
Who can authorize a document, filing, provider engagement, or milestone?
On advisors / service providers
What do lawyers, CPAs, bookkeepers, TA providers, or co-op developers need to see and attest to?
What must remain under professional authority, not ICN's?
On patronage / internal capital (only if opened)
Where do internal capital account / patronage / allocation records first enter the workflow?
What is governance evidence vs. accounting record vs. professional execution? What should ICN never touch?
On anti-software boundaries & next step
What must stay human / process-first? What should expire? Never be public? Need explicit consent?
Smallest fictional / sanitized formation-to-governance rehearsal worth doing? Who else should be in that room?
When critique lands, do not defend. Capture the objection. Translate it into a design question. Ask what would make the thing safer or more useful. The meeting's value is calibration, not persuasion.

Claims NOT to make

  • Not replacing Launch.
  • Not replacing TA providers, lawyers, CPAs, bookkeepers, or co-op developers.
  • Not production-ready. Not externally audited. Not legally / regulatorily certified.
  • Not a live federation. Not an end-to-end federation lifecycle.
  • Not a formally committed cooperative pilot. NYCN is an active partnership track. Launch is a discovery conversation, not a pilot.
  • Not a live member app. Not a live steward cockpit dashboard. Not a signed production appliance.
  • Not accounting software. Not payment / banking / wallet software.
  • Receipts alone do not prove legitimacy. Authority shortcuts must label themselves.
  • Vocabulary: settlement, obligation, allocation, unit, position, receipt, provenance, evidence. Avoid the forbidden financial / crypto vocabulary listed in the Thursday brief.
  • Community-infra (Matrix / n8n / bridge / etc.) is a separate workstream — do not mention unless asked.
  • No real Launch, NYCN, or private partner data has been or will be put in git.
