# ACCES-VR Self-Employment Plan — In motion

**Program:** [acces.nysed.gov/vr](https://www.acces.nysed.gov/vr)

This is operational follow-through, not a grant application. ACCES-VR funds NY-state residents with disabilities pursuing self-employment by paying for services in an approved business plan — up to **$15,000** for inventory, equipment, licenses, advertising, supplies, legal/accounting, and insurance.

## What ACCES-VR funds (eligible categories)

Per NY State policy, ACCES-VR self-employment plans can fund:
- Initial business-related equipment (computers, hardware, software licenses)
- Initial inventory
- Initial business licenses + fees
- Advertising + marketing
- Office supplies
- Legal services (cooperative formation, contracts)
- Accounting services (especially SSDI-compatible income planning)
- Business-related insurance

What it does NOT fund:
- Ongoing salary / wages to the participant
- Personal living expenses
- Speculative investments

## Compatibility with other funding

ACCES-VR is a **state vocational rehab program**, not federal grant. It's compatible with:
- ✓ Grants from foundations (NLnet, Sovereign Tech, Mozilla, etc.) — these go to ICN/fiscal sponsor, not personal income
- ✓ GitHub Sponsors recurring income (provided it stays under SGA threshold)
- ✓ Cooperative formation as a separate entity

It is NOT compatible with:
- ✗ Personal income that pushes you over SSDI's SGA threshold ($1,690/mo in 2026)
- ✗ Other federal vocational rehab programs (you can only be in one)
