# NGI Sargasso — EU Partner Search

**Apply at:** [ngisargasso.eu](https://ngisargasso.eu/)
**Status:** ⚠️ Multi-month cultivation before application is possible

## Constraint

NGI Sargasso funds **transatlantic cooperation** for Next Generation Internet technologies. The structural rule:

- The **applicant must be an EU entity** (in EU or Horizon Europe associated country)
- The **consortium must include** one US or Canada entity
- ICN team is in NY (US) — we cannot apply alone; we need an EU partner who applies with us as the US-side collaborator

This is fundamentally a **relationship play**, not a write-and-submit play.

## Outreach approach

This is a multi-month effort. The structure:

### Stage 1 — Soft introduction (months 1–2)
- Identify which projects have published research or talks within the last 6 months that overlap with ICN's substrate
- Engage via their open community channels (Matrix, Discord, mailing list, GitHub Discussions)
- Comment substantively on their work; offer technical collaboration on specific issues
- **Not** "we want to do a grant together" — that's premature

### Stage 2 — Concrete collaboration proposal (months 2–4)
- After establishing relationship, propose: "what if we built a small bridge between your project and ours?"
- Examples:
  - Decidim ↔ ICN integration: Decidim's deliberation surface produces ICN-format governance receipts
  - CommonsCloud ↔ ICN: CommonsCloud-hosted services discover and federate via ICN's protocol
  - Karrot ↔ ICN: Karrot's group coordination uses ICN's settlement primitives for shared resource allocation
- Build a demo; co-author a blog post or talk

### Stage 3 — Joint Sargasso application (month 4+)
- Once collaboration is real, structure a Sargasso application together
- EU partner is applicant; ICN is US-side collaborator
- Funded work is the *bridge* between the two systems, not unilateral ICN work
- Submit at the next Sargasso open call

## Why this matters even if Sargasso fails

The EU partner relationship is valuable independent of any single grant:
- European cooperatives are early adopters of cooperative-tech (esp. Spain, Italy, Germany)
- Federation across EU + US is exactly the federation ICN's spec ladder describes
- Joint speaking opportunities at FOSDEM, 38C3, OuiShare Fest, etc.
- Stronger NLnet applications too (NLnet looks favorably on projects with European collaborators even when geographic priority is non-EU)
